Use of this website

Use this site and its forms only for real requests about your own business. Send only information you have the right to share.

Services

We design new websites, rebuild existing ones, look after them on our care plan, and offer a free website check. We also offer Google Business Profile Setup, Local SEO, and Logo and Brand Kit.

  • The free preview. We build your preview first, and it's ready in 3 days. You decide after you've seen it. Tell us what's off and we'll revise it. If you don't like it, we delete it, and we won't keep calling you.
  • The care plan. Care is optional. It covers hosting, security, backups and small changes, done within 2 business days of your text. Care is month to month.
  • Cancelling care. Give us 30 days notice by call or text. When care ends, we hand you the site files, and the web address is already in your name. After that you no longer get help or updates from us: we stop hosting the site and making changes, and you can host the files yourself or with whoever you choose.
  • Without care. If you skip care from the start, we hand you the files to host yourself.
  • Your part. You're responsible for your business details on the site, like your hours and services. While you're on care, we update them when you text us.

Quotes and prices

Every business is different. You get your price with your free preview. Custom work and extras get a price before we start, and we never bill by the hour.

Payments

When you like the preview, we confirm the price we agree with you, you pay, and we finish the site and launch it. Bigger changes after launch get a price before we start.

The monthly price for care is the one we agree with you after your preview.
