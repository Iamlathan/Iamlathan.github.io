Who you're working with

Granite City Digital is a sole proprietorship run by Lathan in Mount Airy, North Carolina. When these terms say I or me, that's who it means, and you means the business owner I'm building for.

By asking for a free sample, or paying for a site, you agree to these terms.

The free sample

I build your sample first. You decide after you've seen it. Tell me what's off and I'll revise it.

If you don't like it, I delete it, and I won't keep calling you.

Prices and paying

  • Starter: $150 one time to build.
  • Pro: from $599 one time. I give you the exact quote before I start.
  • Custom work and extras: quoted before I start, never billed by the hour.

When you like the sample, I confirm the price, you pay, and I finish the site and launch it. Bigger changes after launch get a price before I start.

Your domain and your site

Your web address (yourbusiness.com) is included and registered in your name. It's yours, and it stays yours if you leave.

Once you've paid for the build, the site is yours too. If you ever leave, the site goes with you.

The care plan

Care is optional. Starter care is $25 a month. Pro care is $49 a month with same day priority updates.

It covers hosting, security, backups and small changes whenever you text me, plus a monthly website health report. Your monthly rate never goes up.

Care is month to month, with no contract.

Cancelling care

Give me 30 days' notice by text or email. When care ends, I hand you the site files, and the domain is already in your name.

Once you cancel the care plan, you no longer get help or updates from me. I stop hosting the site and making changes. You can host the files yourself or with whoever you choose.

Without care from the start, I hand you the files to host yourself.

What you send me

Send only photos, logos and words you have the right to use. You're responsible for your business details on the site, like prices and hours, and while you're on care I update them when you text me.

What I can and can't promise

I build and look after every site with care, but I can't promise a site will never go down or will land in a certain spot on Google.

I'm not responsible for problems caused by outside services, like hosting, domain or form companies, or for business lost during an outage. If something I did goes wrong, tell me and I'll fix it. If it can't be sorted out, the most I owe you is what you paid me for that work.

North Carolina law

These terms follow the laws of North Carolina. If you're ever unhappy with something, talk to me first. Most things get sorted out with a phone call.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, I'll update this page and the date at the top. A change never alters a price you already agreed to.
