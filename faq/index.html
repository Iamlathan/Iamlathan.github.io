The basics

What does Granite City Digital actually do?

We design and build websites, set up Google Business Profiles, help with local SEO and make logos. If you'd like, we keep your site running after launch too.

Who do you work with?

Local businesses across the United States, from contractors and cleaners to salons, shops and restaurants.

How fast do I see results?

Your free preview is ready in 3 days, with your business name, your services and a clear way for customers to reach you.

Do I have to commit long term?

No. The preview is free, and you decide after you've seen it. Care is month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days notice.

How do I get started?

Fill out the short form or call or text us, and tell us what you do and where you work. We take it from there.

Your website

How long does a website take?

Your free preview is ready in 3 days. Once you say go, we finish the site and put it live.

Do I own the website when it's done?

Yes. The website is yours, and the web address is registered in your name.

Can you rebuild the site I already have?

Yes. Send us the address and we'll tell you straight whether to fix it or start fresh, and you still see a free preview first.

Do you handle hosting and updates?

Yes, with our optional care plan. Without it, we hand you the files to host yourself.

Why us

What makes Granite City different?

You see your website before paying anything, you own it with the web address in your name, and you talk directly to the one building it.

Where do you work?

We work in the United States. Everything happens by call or text, wherever your business is.

How do I know if my site is costing me customers?

Ask for our free website check, which looks at your site the way a customer would and tells you plainly what stops them. Your result shows right on the same page, and if the automatic check can't open your site, we look at it by hand and get back to you by call or text.

Our work

Do you only build for the businesses in your samples?

No. Our sample designs use made up business names to show the range, and your site is built around your business.

Will my site look like your samples?

No. Every site gets a look and colors made for it, with your photos and your services.

About us

Who is behind Granite City Digital?

Granite City Digital is a web design studio started by someone who wants local businesses to win, and the person who plans your site also builds it and looks after it. Meet the team on our About page.

Getting in touch

How do I reach you?

Call or text (743) 222-0985, or fill out the short form.

Is the preview really free?

Yes. You see your website before paying anything.

What happens after I ask for a preview?

We build your free preview and you look it over. Tell us what's off and we revise it, and you decide once it's right.

Do I have to be ready to buy?

No. The preview is there so you can decide with the real site in front of you.

Before you decide

I already have a website. Why rebuild it?

If customers can't find what they need, or the site is hard to use, they go somewhere else. Our free website check shows you what a customer runs into.

Will this get me more customers?

Every site we build puts your services up front and makes you easy to call. Look through our sample designs, then judge your own free preview.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different. You get your price with your free preview.

What if a web designer let me down before?

That's why you see your website first: you decide with the real thing in front of you, and once it's done, the site and its web address are yours.

Care and your part

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, what you do and where you work, plus any photos, hours and services you have handy. Call or text them to (743) 222-0985, or use the form, and we organize the rest.

What is the monthly for?

It pays for our care plan, which is optional and month to month: hosting, security, backups and small changes, done within 2 business days of your text.

What if I cancel?

Give us 30 days notice by call or text. When care ends, we hand you the site files, and the web address is already in your name. After that we stop hosting and updating the site, so help and changes are up to you or whoever you choose. The full rules are in the terms.

Still have a question?

Call or text us with it, or start with your free preview and see for yourself.
