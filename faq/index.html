Plain answers about time, price, owning your site and leaving. If yours isn't here, text me at (743) 222-0985.

Getting started

What happens before your site goes live.

  • How long does it take?

    Your free sample comes in 3 days, guaranteed. Once you OK it, the site is live within a week. I reply to your texts and calls the same day the whole way through.

  • What do I need to give you?

    Your business name, what you do and where you work, and any photos you have. Hours, services and prices help if you have them handy.

    Text them to me at (743) 222-0985 or use the form on the home page, and I'll organize the rest.

  • What if I don't like the sample?

    Tell me what's off and I'll revise it. If it's still not right, I delete the sample, and I won't keep calling you.

  • Do I need to know anything about computers?

    No. You never log in to anything. I take care of the technical side from start to finish.

  • My business is only on Facebook. Is that a problem?

    Not at all. Send me your business name and your Facebook page, and I start from what's on it.

    Keep the page. Your website gives customers one place with your services, your hours and a button to call you, and Google setup is part of the build.

  • Can you fix the website I already have?

    Yes. Send me the address and I'll tell you straight whether to fix it or start fresh. A rebuild costs the same $150, and you see the free sample first, the same as with a new site.

    Not sure yet? Start with a free website check.

Price and paying

No contracts, no hourly bills, no surprise invoices.

  • What will I actually pay?

    Starter is $150 once to build, plus $25 a month if you want me to host and update it. Pro starts at $599 one time, call or text for a quote, plus $49 a month for care. Your web address is included, registered in your name.

    Need something bigger than Pro? That's custom work, and I quote it before I start. See pricing.

  • What is the monthly for?

    Hosting, security, backups and small changes whenever you text me, like new hours, prices or photos, plus a monthly website health report.

    Starter care is $25 a month. Pro care is $49 a month with same day priority updates. It's optional, it's month to month, and your monthly rate never goes up.

  • Do you do logos?

    Logos aren't one of my set services, so there's no set price for one. If you need a logo, text me what you have in mind and I'll give you a straight yes or no.

  • Do you only do websites?

    No. I also write resumes and cover letters from $25 and build Excel and Google Sheets tools from $20 for people around Surry County who hear about me through my website work.

Owning it and leaving

Your site and your web address stay yours.

  • Who owns the site and the web address?

    You do. The domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave, the site goes with you.

  • What if I cancel?

    Care is month to month. Give me 30 days' notice, and I hand you the files. The domain is already in your name.

    Once care ends, I stop hosting and updating the site, so help and changes after that are up to you or whoever you choose. The full rules are in the terms.
