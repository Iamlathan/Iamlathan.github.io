Read your tire size

It's printed on the side of every tire. Tap each part to see what it means, then read the whole thing to us when you call.

/
225
Width. The tire is 225 millimeters across, from sidewall to sidewall.
60
Aspect ratio. The sidewall is 60 percent as tall as the tire is wide.
R
Construction. R means radial, which is how nearly every car tire is built today.
17
Wheel size. This tire fits a 17 inch wheel.
98
Load index. A code for how much weight each tire can carry.
H
Speed rating. A letter code for the top speed the tire is built for.

The size shown is an example. Yours has its own numbers.

After a wreck

It's your car. Where it gets fixed is your call.

  1. Call or bring it by

    If it's safe to drive, bring it in. If it isn't, call and we'll talk about getting it here.

  2. We look it over

    We go over the damage with you, panel by panel, and write up what we find.

  3. Insurance, if you want

    Bring your claim number. We work with your adjuster, or you handle it yourself.

Straight answers

Do I have to use the shop my insurer names?
Read your policy, but it's your car, and you can ask any shop to look at it. Bring your claim number and we'll talk it through.
Can you match my car's color?
Yes. We start from the paint code on your car and blend into the panels next to the repair.
What size tires do I need?
It's on the side of the tires you have now. Use the size reader above, or read it to us over the phone.
My pressure light is on. Is it a flat?
Not always. A cold morning can set it off. Have the pressure checked, and if one tire keeps dropping, bring it in.
