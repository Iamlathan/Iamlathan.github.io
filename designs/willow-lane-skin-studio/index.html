Your visit

What a visit looks like

  1. We sit and talk.

    I ask about your skin, what you use on it and what you'd like to change. Ask me anything.

  2. I work with today's skin.

    Skin shifts with the weather, sleep and stress, so the facial changes with it. Bend sits in the high desert, where the air runs dry, so we'll talk about simple ways to keep moisture in.

  3. I write down a simple routine for home.

    It starts with what you already have. I'm not here to sell you a shelf of products.

  4. Come back when it suits you.

    Monthly, or with the seasons. There's no package to sign up for.

Before your visit
