What a visit looks like

  1. We sit and talk.

    I ask about your skin, what you use on it and what you'd like to change. Ask me anything.

  2. I work with today's skin.

    Skin shifts with the weather, sleep and stress, so the facial shifts too.

  3. I write down what I'd do at home.

    It starts with what you already have. I'm not here to sell you a shelf of products.

  4. Come back when it suits you.

    Every few weeks or a few times a year. There's no package to sign up for.
