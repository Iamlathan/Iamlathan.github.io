Questions

Plumbing questions

A pipe burst. What do I do first?

Shut off the main water valve. In most homes it sits where the main line comes in, often in the basement, the crawl space or near the water heater. Then call.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing?

Yes. A steady drip wastes water every day, and the cause is often a worn part inside the faucet.

Why does my drain keep clogging?

A clog that comes back means something is building up farther down the line. Clearing the top of the drain only buys a little time.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

Not a firm one. We see the job first, then give you the price. Tell us what is going on and we will talk it through.

How do I keep pipes from freezing?

On hard freezes, let a faucet on an outside wall drip and open the cabinet doors under sinks.
