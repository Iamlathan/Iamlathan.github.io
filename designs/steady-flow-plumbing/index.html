Plumbing questions

A pipe burst. What do I do first?

Shut off the main water valve. In most homes it sits where the water line comes in, often in the crawl space, the basement or near the water heater. Then call.

Is a dripping faucet worth fixing?

Yes. A steady drip wastes water every day, and the cause is often a worn part inside the faucet.

Why does my drain keep clogging?

A clog that comes back means something is building up further down the line. Clearing the top of the drain only buys a little time.

Can you give me a price over the phone?

No. We see the job first, then give you the price. Tell us what is going on and we will tell you the next step.

How do I keep pipes from freezing?

On hard freezes, let a faucet on an outside wall drip, open the cabinet doors under sinks, and keep crawl space vents closed.
