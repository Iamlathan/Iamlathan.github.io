Fire mitigation

Many Rim Country homes and businesses are built near dense forest and thick brush. We are trained to provide fire mitigation, and there are four ways we make a property defensible and fire wise.

  • Thinning and removing brush and ladder fuels
  • Trimming and elevating trees
  • Removing fallen leaves, pine needles and other ground fuels
  • Creating a fire break around the perimeter of the home

ISA Certified Arborist

Tree Crafters is ISA arborist certified. ISA is the International Society of Arboriculture.

Being certified gives us “the skills and knowledge to make sound tree care decisions” so we can “preserve, care for, and remove trees in the best way possible”, tailored to the Rim Country area and its ecosystem.
