Name the trouble

Start with what you can see, hear or feel. These are the problems we fix.

  • It will not start

    The key turns and nothing happens, or it cranks and never catches. If it cannot be driven, ask for a tow to our door.

  • A warning light is on

    The check engine light and the other warning lights on the dash.

  • It is running hot

    Cooling trouble, whether the gauge is climbing or the car is losing coolant.

  • It misfires, shakes or stalls

    Engine trouble you can feel from the driver seat.

  • It is leaking

    Fluid on the ground under the car.

Not sure what it is? Call or text and tell us what you notice.
