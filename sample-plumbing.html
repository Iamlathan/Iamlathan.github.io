Questions

Do you really answer at night?

Yes. After hours calls go to whoever is on call, usually Dale. You hear the price for coming out before anyone gets in the truck.

How do I know what it will cost?

Starting prices are listed above. Once we see the problem, you get the full price in writing, and work starts only after you say yes.

Can I send a photo first?

Please do. Text a photo to (336) 555-0147 with a line about what is happening. We can often tell you what it is before we come.

A pipe just burst. What do I do?

Shut off the main valve where the water line enters the house, or at the pressure tank if you are on a well. Turn off the water heater. Then call (336) 555-0147.
