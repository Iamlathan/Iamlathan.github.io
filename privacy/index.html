Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Granite City Digital is a web design business. This policy explains what we collect through this website, how we use it, who else handles it, and the choices you have. When it says we or us, it means Granite City Digital.

Information we collect

  • Free preview form. Your name, phone number, business name, what kind of work you do and your city and state. Whether you have a site and its address if you do, what your business does best, the main problem right now, when you want your website, and anything else you tell us.
  • Contact form. Your name, business name, email address and phone number, your website address if you give one, and your message.
  • Homepage contact form. Your name, email address and phone number, your website address if you give one, and your message if you write one.
  • Change request form on the client portal page. Your name, phone number, your business or website, what you'd like changed, and how soon.
  • Free website check. Your business name, your website address or Facebook link, and a phone number if you choose to give one. When the automatic check runs, we also receive its results: your score, the result lines you see, the points for each item, the final address your site landed on, and the screenshot link.
  • Your two checkbox answers. Every form that asks for a phone number also records whether you agreed to get text messages about your request, appointments, project updates and related service messages, and that you agreed to this policy and our Terms.
  • Calls and texts. If you call or text us, we keep that conversation so we can help you and remember what you asked for.
  • Email. If you write to us directly, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them to reply and to do the work you ask about.
  • Technical details. Like any website, the services that deliver this site see your IP address and basic browser details. We describe each one under How we share information.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you by call or text, or by email if you wrote to us or sent a message through a contact form.
  • To build your free preview and do the work you ask about.
  • To make the changes you request through the client portal.
  • To follow up on your free website check. If you leave the page while the check is running, the details you typed are still sent to us so we can follow up.

We don't use your information for ads, and there are no ad trackers on this site.

Text messages

If you check the text message box on one of our forms, we may text you at the number you gave about your inquiry, appointments, project updates and related service messages. How often varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to stop the texts; you can withdraw your text consent this way at any time. Agreeing to texts is never a condition of buying anything from us. We don't send marketing texts. We don't sell or share mobile numbers or text consent with anyone for their marketing.

How we share information

We don't sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with the services below, so the site works.

  • Web3Forms delivers form entries to us. When you press send, your browser sends what you typed to Web3Forms, which emails it to us. Web3Forms keeps submissions for up to three years under its own privacy policy, and may pass the sender's IP address to spam filters.
  • Microlink (microlink.io) takes the screenshot for the free website check. Only the website address goes to Microlink, never your name or phone number. Facebook and Instagram links are not sent to it at all. Microlink loads that page, keeps a cached copy, and hosts the screenshot at a public microlink.io link, so anyone with that address can see it. Please don't enter a private or password protected address. Microlink also sees your IP address, and its own privacy policy says it logs use to find errors and improve response times.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on our sample design pages. When you open one, the fonts come from Google, which sees your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security, as its GitHub Pages documentation explains.
  • Your phone provider handles calls and texts with us under its own policies.

Data security

We keep what you send in our own records and share it only as this policy describes. No way of sending information over the internet is perfectly secure, so please don't send passwords or payment details through our forms.

Your choices

  • Ask us to delete it. Call or text us and tell us what you'd like deleted. We'll delete it from our records and let you know when it's done. For copies held by Web3Forms or Microlink, we'll ask them to delete what they can, or point you to their own process.
  • Reach us your way. You never have to use a form. You can call or text us instead.
  • Browser storage. When the automatic tool reaches its daily limit, the free check page saves one small note in your browser holding only that day's date, so it skips straight to the step where we look by hand. It isn't used for tracking, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Children's privacy

This site is for business owners. It isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
