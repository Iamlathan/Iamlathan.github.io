Who I am

Granite City Digital is a web design business run by Lathan in Mount Airy, North Carolina. When this page says I or me, that's who it means.

Questions about your information? Text or call (743) 222-0985 or email lathan@granitecitydigital.me.

What you send through the forms

The free sample form asks for your name, your business name, your phone number, what you do and where, and your current website if you have one.

The free website check forms ask for your business name and your website address or Facebook link, plus a way to reach you: your name and a phone number or email on the home page, or an optional phone number on the free check page.

When you press send, your browser sends what you typed to Web3Forms, a form service, which emails it to me. Web3Forms keeps form submissions for up to three years under its own privacy policy, and may pass the sender's IP address to spam filters.

On the free check page, that send also includes the check results: your score, the result lines you see, the points for each check, the final address your site landed on, and the Microlink screenshot link. If you leave the page while the check is still running, the details you typed are still sent to me so I can follow up.

I use what you send only to reply to you and to do the work you asked about.

The free website check and Microlink

On the free check page, the website address you type is sent from your browser to Microlink (microlink.io), a screenshot service. Only the address goes to Microlink, not your name or your contact details.

Microlink loads that page, keeps a cached copy, and hosts the screenshot at a public microlink.io link, so anyone who has that link can see the screenshot. It's a picture of a public web page, but please don't enter a private or password protected address.

Like any website your browser contacts, Microlink also sees your IP address. Its own privacy policy says it logs API use to find errors and improve response times.

When the free automatic checks run out for the day, the free check page saves one small note in your browser (local storage) holding only that day's date, so for the rest of that day it skips straight to the check I do by hand. It isn't used for tracking.

Fonts and hosting

The text on this site uses fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser downloads them from Google, so Google sees your IP address and browser details when it does. See Google's privacy policy.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of every visitor for security, as its GitHub Pages documentation explains.

What I don't do

  • I don't sell, rent or trade your information.
  • There are no ad trackers on this site, and I don't use cookies for ads or tracking.
  • I don't share what you send with anyone except the services named on this page, and only so the site works.

Calls, texts and emails

If you call, text or email me, I keep that conversation so I can help you and remember what you asked for. Your phone and email providers handle those messages under their own policies.

The Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn links in the footer take you to those sites, which have their own privacy rules.

Asking me to delete your information

Want me to delete what I have about you? Text or call (743) 222-0985 or email lathan@granitecitydigital.me and tell me what you'd like deleted. I'll delete it from my email and records and let you know when it's done.

For copies held by Web3Forms or Microlink, I'll ask them to delete what they can, or point you to their own process.

Children

This site is for business owners. It isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect their information.
