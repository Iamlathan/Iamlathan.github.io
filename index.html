Good to know

A few common questions.

Why is the price so low?

No office, no employees, no ad budget. I build sites one at a time from Mount Airy, and the founding client prices are on purpose while I build a local portfolio.

What if I don't like the sample?

Tell me what's off and I'll revise it. If it's still not right, we part ways and you owe nothing.

Hosting, security, backups, and small changes like new hours, prices or photos. You text me, I handle it. Cancel anytime.

No. You never log in to anything. I take care of the technical side from start to finish.

Usually. Send me the address and I'll tell you honestly whether it's worth fixing or faster to start fresh.

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave, the site goes with you.

Websites are the main thing. I also write resumes and cover letters from $25 and build Excel and Google Sheets tools from $20, mostly for people around Surry County who hear about me through the website work.
