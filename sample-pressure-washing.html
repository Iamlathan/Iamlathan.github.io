Questions we get a lot

Will it hurt my siding?

Not the way we do it. Siding gets low pressure and a cleaning mix that does the work. The only thing that gets real pressure is concrete.

Do I need to be home?

No. We need an outside spigot and the dogs inside. We'll text you a before and after when we're done.

Is the stuff you spray safe for plants?

We wet everything down first and rinse after. Keep pets off the grass until it's dry and you're fine.

How do I pay?

After the job, once you've walked around and looked at it. Cash, check, card or Venmo.
