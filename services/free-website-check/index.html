The real problem

You never see it the way they do.

You know your business, so your own site always makes sense to you. A customer meets it cold, often in a hurry, and leaves at the first thing that gets in the way.

The check shows you that first visit, in plain words and without a sales pitch.

You get

  • A score out of 100
  • The top problems, in plain words
  • A picture of your site next to one of our sample designs

How the check works

The check is quick, plain and free.

It runs right away.

The automatic check opens your site, looks at six things and shows your result right there on the same page.

The problems, named plainly.

Your result lists what gets in a customer's way, and what you do about it is your call.

Facebook pages get checked too, by hand.

Facebook and Instagram need a login the automatic check can't get past, so we read those pages ourselves and reply by call or text.

Your details stay with us.

Only the site address goes to the checking service, and your name and phone number come to us alone.

What we look at

Six things the check looks at.

  • It loads

    The address opens a working page, not an error.

  • Secure address

    The address starts with https, so browsers don't warn people away.

  • Fits small screens

    The page fits the screen instead of showing a shrunk desktop layout.

  • Call button

    Your number is a button that rings you when someone taps it.

  • Hours shown

    People can see when you're open without calling first.

  • Load time

    An estimate of how long the page takes to open, since people give up on slow pages.

FAQ

Questions about the check.

Can you check my site for me?

Yes. The free website check opens your site and looks at six things, from load time to your call button.

Is the check really free?

Yes, and you see your result right away.

What if my business is only on Facebook?

Send us your Facebook page link. The automatic check can't open pages behind a login, so we look at it by hand and get back to you by call or text.

What happens after the check?

That's up to you. If you'd like a better site, we build your free preview in 3 days, and you decide after you've seen it.
