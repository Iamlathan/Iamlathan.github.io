What's included

Six things in your brand kit.

  • Your logo

  • Versions for every background

FAQ

Questions about your logo.

What files do I get?

SVG and PNG for screens and a PDF for print, in full color, black and white, plus your one page brand sheet.

Do you check trademarks?

No. Checking a name or logo against registered marks is legal work, and the federal trademark office suggests an attorney for it. We're happy to send your logo files to your attorney.

How many logo ideas will I see?

We show you a few directions and refine the one you pick.

Can you clean up the logo I already have?

Yes. If you like your logo but the files are blurry, we redraw it clean and build the kit around it.

Who owns the logo?

You do, once it's paid for.

How much does it cost?

Every business is different. You get your price with your free preview.
